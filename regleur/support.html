Régleur support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach a real person. Régleur is made by one independent developer, so a reply may take a couple of working days.

Contact us

Email hello at celadora.net. You can also message us through Ko-fi, though email is the channel we actually monitor. Please include:

  • Your device model and iOS or iPadOS version.
  • The Régleur version (Settings, then About, in the app).
  • What you expected, and what happened instead.

You do not need to send serial numbers, photos or recordings. If you choose to attach something, we use it only to answer you. See the privacy policy for how we handle support email.

Measuring

How do I get a good reading?

Measure in a quiet room. Place the phone on a soft surface such as a folded towel or a mouse pad, and rest the watch against the phone where the in-app guide shows the microphone. Keep still while Régleur listens; the "give or take" narrows the longer it listens.

Régleur says the watch is too quiet.

Move to a quieter room, try another side of the watch case (the caseback or near the crown often carries the tick best), and make sure the watch touches the phone. Some watches are simply very quiet. An external contact microphone can help.

How accurate are the numbers?

A phone microphone is not a contact sensor. Rate and beat error are usually reliable. Amplitude is an estimate and is most useful for comparing readings of the same watch on the same phone over time. Régleur is a helpful guide, not a substitute for a watchmaker's instrument or advice.

The measured beat rate differs from my watch's profile.

Movements are sometimes listed with a different beat rate, or a profile was entered by hand. Régleur shows what it measured and offers to update the profile. Nothing else changes.

Permissions

Why does Régleur need the microphone?

To hear your watch tick. Audio is analyzed live on your device and is never recorded or uploaded. To turn access on or off, open the Settings app, tap Régleur, then Microphone.

Why does Régleur ask for the camera?

Only if you choose to photograph a watch for your collection. You can pick existing photos instead; the photo picker shows only the photos you select, so Régleur never needs access to your whole library.

Subscriptions and purchases

How do I cancel or change Régleur Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Régleur. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Apple's guide: If you want to cancel a subscription from Apple.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot see or issue payments ourselves.

I bought Pro but it is not active.

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe, then use Restore Purchases in the app. If it still does not work, email us with the date of purchase (no receipts or card details needed).

Your data

Where is my collection stored?

On your device. We do not run a server that receives your watches, photos or readings. Your data is included in your device backups according to your own iCloud Backup or computer backup settings.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual watches or readings inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on the device. If a future version offers iCloud sync and you turn it on, you can also remove the synced copy in your device's iCloud storage settings. Details are in the privacy policy.

Does Régleur sync between my devices?

Not yet. iCloud sync is planned as an optional feature that stores your data in your own private iCloud database, which we cannot access.
