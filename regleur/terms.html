The numbers are estimates

Régleur estimates how a watch is running from the sound your device's microphone picks up. Results depend on the device, the watch, the room and placement, and amplitude is an estimate. Régleur is a guide for enthusiasts, not a certified measuring instrument, and its readings are not a valuation, an authentication or servicing advice. Ask a qualified watchmaker before relying on a reading for repairs, purchases or sales.
