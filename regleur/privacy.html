Régleur privacy policy

It covers the Régleur app for iPhone; the Celadora Studios privacy policy covers our website and sets the baseline all our apps follow.

Summary

Régleur sends nothing to us on its own. We do not operate any server that receives information from the app. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking code, and no third-party software development kit that collects data.

Audio stays on your device. When you measure, Régleur listens to your watch through the microphone and analyzes the sound live, in memory, without saving it. The one exception is a support capture: if you need our help with a measurement, you can record your watch for up to one minute and send us the file yourself. Nothing is recorded until you start a capture, nothing is sent unless you send it, and the app deletes the recording from your iPhone when you finish (section 3.6).

Your collection stays on your device. Watches, photos, readings and notes you add are stored on your iPhone. We cannot see them. If you turn on the optional iCloud backup, a copy is also kept in your own private iCloud account, which we cannot access.

Apple handles payments. Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. We never receive your name, email address or payment details from a purchase.

If this ever changes, you will know first. Before any new feature sends personal data off your device, we will update this policy, tell you in the app, and ask for your consent where the law requires it (see section 15).

The only personal data we receive is what you choose to send us, for example when you email us for help or send us a support capture (section 6).

3. Information that stays on your device

Régleur works with the following information. All of it is processed and stored only on your device, except a support capture that you choose to send us (section 3.6) (and, as described in section 5, in your own device backups and, for four display settings and the optional collection backup, in your own iCloud). We do not receive it and cannot access it.

3.1 Microphone audio

When you start a measurement, Régleur asks for permission to use the microphone. It reads the sound in short pieces, analyzes them in your device's memory to find the watch's ticks, and then discards them. A measurement never writes audio to a file and never sends it anywhere. Measuring stops when you stop it or leave the screen. The microphone is used only while you measure and while you record a support capture (section 3.6).

What Régleur does save with a measurement, on your device, is the result: the date, rate, beat error, beat rate, amplitude estimate, the timing of each detected beat, an averaged outline of the tick shape used to draw the scope view, the measurement duration, the name of the input used (for example "iPhone Microphone") and the app version. These are numbers describing your watch; they cannot be played back as a recording and do not capture speech or room sound. The "play" feature in a result plays simulated clicks generated from those numbers, not a recording. The Demo Watch uses simulated sound only.

You can turn microphone access off at any time in the Settings app (Régleur, then Microphone). Without it, you can still use the Demo Watch and your collection.

3.2 Camera and photos

  • Camera. Régleur asks for camera access only when you choose to photograph a watch. You can refuse and still use the app.
  • Photo library. Choosing existing photos uses Apple's photo picker, which runs outside the app and gives Régleur only the photos you select. Régleur does not request access to your photo library.
  • What is stored. Régleur stores resized copies of your chosen photos (at most 2048 pixels on the long side, plus a small thumbnail). These copies are created from the image itself and, in current versions, do not carry over the original file's embedded details such as location.
  • Photo cleanup. The optional background cleanup that isolates a watch in a photo uses Apple's on-device Vision technology. Your photo is not uploaded for this. If you clean up a photo, Régleur keeps the earlier version on your device so you can restore it.
  • Saving to Photos. If a version of Régleur lets you save an image to your photo library, iOS will ask your permission first, and the app can only add images, not read your library.

3.3 Your collection

Anything you type into Régleur stays on your device: watch names, brand, model, reference and serial numbers, movement details, notes, and purchase and service dates. If you turn on iCloud backup, a copy of your collection (watches, photos, readings and notes) is also stored in your own private iCloud account (section 5.2). Régleur Pro adds unlimited measurements, your whole collection active at once, full measurement history and Trends, service intervals and every photo backdrop; it does not change where your information is kept. Links you save to documents (such as a datasheet) are stored as text. Please avoid putting other people's personal information in notes.

3.4 Settings, reminders and device details

  • Settings and preferences (such as haptics, measurement length and view options) are stored on your device and, unless you switch settings sync off, in your own iCloud (section 5.2).
  • Free-tier count. The measurement sessions you have used this month are counted on your device, in a record kept in the device's secure keychain for this device only (it is not synced or sent to us). Because the keychain can outlast the app, deleting and reinstalling Régleur may not reset the count.
  • Reminders. Régleur does not send reminders or notifications in this version. If a later version adds service reminders, they will be local notifications scheduled by your device. iOS would ask your permission first, and no reminder data would be sent to us.
  • Device model. Régleur may check your device model on the device itself to show where its microphone is. This is not sent anywhere, unless you include iPhone details in a support capture you send us (section 3.6).
  • Diagnostic messages. If something fails, the app may write a short technical error message to your device's system log. It contains no collection data and stays on your device unless you choose to share diagnostics with Apple (section 5.4).

3.5 Exports, sharing and links

  • Exports and backups. This version has no report, spreadsheet or backup-file export. If a later version adds one, Régleur will create the file on your device and hand it to the share sheet. It would go only where you send it, and the place you send it to would handle it under its own terms.
  • Links you open. Web links (such as a document link or this policy) open in an in-app Safari view. The website you visit receives the usual information any browser sends, such as your IP address. Régleur cannot see your browsing in that view, and Safari keeps it separate from the app.

3.6 Support captures (optional)

If a measurement does not work as expected, Régleur's Settings has a Support section where you can record a support capture and send it to us. It is entirely optional, the rest of the app works the same without it, and it never runs on its own.

  • When it records. Only after you tap to start a capture. It records the microphone for up to one minute (you can stop it sooner), through the same path the app uses to measure. It can pick up voices or other sounds nearby, so record somewhere quiet.
  • What you choose to include. Before anything leaves the app, you choose which parts go into one ZIP file: the recording (the sound the app heard); debug information (what the app measured every half second and the microphone settings); and iPhone information (model identifier such as "iPhone18,2", iOS version, language and region, battery level and state, temperature state and Low Power Mode). The file always includes a short description of itself: the app version and build, the start time and length of the capture, and which parts you chose.
  • What it never contains. Your watches, their names, brands, models or references, notes, photos, your device's name, any advertising or vendor identifier, your Apple Account, your location or your time zone name. If you used an external microphone, its name is replaced with "redacted".
  • How it is sent. Régleur does not upload it. It hands the ZIP to the iOS share sheet, and you decide where it goes, for example by email to us. The app, mail service or other destination you choose handles it under its own terms.
  • On your iPhone. The capture lives in the app's private storage only while the capture screen is open. Régleur deletes it when you finish, close the screen or record again, and removes any leftover file the next time the app opens. It is never part of your collection, your iCloud backup or a measurement.
  • What we do with it. If you send it to us, we use it only to understand and fix the problem you reported, as part of your support request (section 6), and we delete it as described in section 9. We do not use your recording to train or test our measurement engine unless you tell us in writing that we may.

Because this information stays under your control and is never transmitted to us by the app, we do not "collect" it, as Apple and most privacy laws use that word. You are in charge of it: you can view, change, export or delete it at any time in the app.

4. Information we do not collect

Through the app, we do not collect, receive or store:

  • audio recordings, or any sound from your surroundings, except a support capture that you choose to send us (section 3.6);
  • your name, email address, phone number or any account (there is no Régleur account);
  • your location (Régleur does not ask for location access);
  • your contacts, calendar, health or fitness data;
  • advertising identifiers or any identifier used to track you across apps or websites;
  • usage analytics, crash data or diagnostics from our own code (we include no analytics or crash-reporting software), except the debug and iPhone information in a support capture that you choose to send us;
  • payment card or bank details, which Apple handles.

We do not sell personal data, "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising, show ads, or build profiles about you. Régleur does not track you as Apple defines tracking, so it does not show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Régleur does not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. Our App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected". A support capture does not change this: the app itself sends nothing, and a capture reaches us only when you choose to send it to us as a support request. This also stays accurate with iCloud backup on: the backup is stored in your own private iCloud account, and neither it nor anything in it is sent to, or readable by, us.

5. Information handled by Apple

Some features rely on Apple's services. Apple acts on its own account under Apple's Privacy Policy, not on our instructions, and we do not control what Apple collects.

5.1 Downloads, subscriptions and payments

Régleur is distributed through the App Store, and Régleur Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes your Apple Account, payment and billing details. We do not receive your name, email address, payment details or purchase receipts that identify you. The app checks whether you have an active subscription using Apple's StoreKit framework, on your device.

Apple gives us sales and financial reports for running the business, such as the number of downloads and subscriptions, renewals, refunds, proceeds and the country or region of sale. These reports are aggregated and do not tell us who you are. Refund requests go to Apple, which decides them.

5.2 iCloud sync and device backups

Settings sync. Régleur can sync four display preferences between your devices: appearance, startup animation, haptics and the Simple or Detailed Measure view. This uses Apple's iCloud key-value storage in your own iCloud account when you are signed in to iCloud. It is on by default and you can switch it off in Régleur's Settings, iCloud. Your audio is never synced.

Collection backup and restore. Régleur can back up your collection (watches, photos, readings and notes) to the private database of your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit service, so you can get it back after reinstalling Régleur or moving to a new iPhone. It is optional and off until you turn it on in Régleur's Settings, iCloud, and it needs you to be signed in to iCloud. Apple stores the backup under its terms. We, as the developer, cannot access, read or download the contents of your private iCloud database, and nothing is sent to us, so we do not collect it. Audio is never part of a backup.

Turning it off and deleting it. Switch Back Up Collection off in Régleur's Settings, iCloud. You can see and delete individual backups in Régleur's Settings, iCloud, Backups in iCloud. To remove all of it, open your iPhone's iCloud storage settings (Settings, then your name, then iCloud), choose Régleur, and delete its data.

Finding a backup after a reinstall. When Régleur opens with an empty collection and you have not answered before, it looks in your own private iCloud for a Régleur backup and, if it finds one, offers to restore it. This is what lets you get your collection back after reinstalling Régleur or moving to a new iPhone. The check only reads: it does not upload anything, you do not need to have turned backup on first, it does not run while your collection already has watches in it, and nothing about it reaches us. If you are not signed in to iCloud or no backup exists, nothing happens.

Restoring. A restore only ever adds to your collection. It never overwrites or merges into watches that are already on your iPhone, and it asks before it adds anything. If a backup overlaps a collection that is already on the iPhone, Régleur declines and tells you how to proceed (delete and reinstall Régleur, which leaves your backups in iCloud untouched).

Your device backups (iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer) may include Régleur's data, according to your own backup settings.

  • TestFlight. If you join a Régleur beta, Apple shares with us information needed to run the test, such as the name and email address you were invited with or used to join, your device model and operating system version, and any crash reports, screenshots or comments you choose to send. We use this only to run and improve the beta, and delete it within 12 months after the beta ends unless we still need it to answer you.

If you allow it on your device (Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements, "Share With App Developers"), Apple may share crash reports and app usage statistics with developers. Apple provides these to us without identifying you. We use them only to fix bugs and improve stability. You can turn this off at any time in the same place. App Store Connect also shows us aggregated statistics such as the number of installs and sessions, only for people who agreed to share analytics with developers.

8. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. The app shares no user data with any third party. For the limited information we hold (such as support email), we disclose it only:

  • to our email provider, which stores it on our behalf;
  • if required by law, a court order or a valid legal request, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users, the public or us;
  • to a successor if Celadora Studios or Régleur is transferred, for example if the owner moves the business into a company, as long as the successor honors this policy (we will tell you before your information becomes subject to a different policy).

If we ever use a service provider that receives user data, we will require it by contract to protect that data at least as well as this policy does, and to use it only on our instructions.

9. How long we keep information

  • Data on your device: kept until you delete it in the app or delete the app. We hold no copy.
  • Support captures: the ZIP file and its recording are deleted within 90 days after your support request is closed, or sooner if you ask. We keep only a short written note of what we found.
  • Support emails: kept for up to 24 months after our last message in the conversation, then deleted, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or we need them longer to resolve a dispute.
  • Records of privacy requests: kept for 24 months, as some laws require, and then deleted.
  • Beta information: deleted within 12 months after the beta ends.
  • Reports from Apple: kept in App Store Connect under Apple's retention rules; they do not identify you.

10. Security

Régleur's data lives inside the app's private storage area on your device, which other apps cannot read. iOS encrypts this storage when your device is protected by a passcode, so a strong passcode is the most important protection you can add. Because we hold no copy of your collection, a breach of our systems could not expose it.

For the little we do hold, such as support email, we use an email account protected by a strong password and two-factor authentication. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affecting your personal data occurs, we will notify you and the relevant authorities where the law requires.

11. International transfers

We are based in the United States, and our email provider may store messages in the United States or other countries. If you contact us from outside the United States, your message travels to us there because you sent it to us. Where a law treats this as a transfer that needs a safeguard, we rely on the fact that it is necessary to answer your request (for example GDPR Art. 49(1)(b)), and on our provider's own transfer safeguards, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses, where available. The United States does not have a single national privacy law equivalent to the GDPR; the safeguards in this policy apply to you wherever you live. Contact us for more details.

12. Your rights and choices

12.1 Control inside the app and on your device

  • View and edit your collection in the app.
  • Switch settings sync and collection backup off in Régleur's Settings, iCloud, and delete backups there or in your iPhone's iCloud storage settings.
  • Delete a watch, a photo or a reading in the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the device.
  • Turn microphone and camera permissions on or off in the Settings app.
  • Choose which parts go into a support capture, or discard it without sending. A capture you do not send never leaves your iPhone.
  • Stop sharing diagnostics with developers in Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

12.2 Rights over information we hold

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to: know whether we hold personal data about you and get a copy (access, portability); have it corrected; have it deleted; restrict or object to how we use it; withdraw consent you gave; not be discriminated against for using these rights; and appeal our decision on your request. We offer these rights to everyone, wherever you live.

To make a request, email hello at celadora.net with the subject "Privacy request". Tell us the email address you used to contact us, so we can find your messages. We do not need any other identification, and we will never ask for your password. We will respond within one month, free of charge, or sooner where your local law sets a shorter deadline (for example 15 days for access requests in Brazil and 10 days in South Korea), and tell you if a complex request needs longer where the law allows it (up to two further months under the GDPR). If we cannot fulfil a request, we will explain why. In most cases our honest answer will be that we hold no data about you except your email to us and any support capture you sent with it. You may use an authorized agent where your law allows; we may ask the agent for proof of your permission.

Appeals. If you disagree with our response, reply with "Appeal" in the subject line. We will review it and answer within 45 days, and tell you how to contact your regulator if you are still not satisfied.

Minor changes (for example, clearer wording, a new contact address, or a correction that does not reduce your protections) take effect when we publish them.
