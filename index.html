Régleur

Know your movement

In testing

Rest a mechanical watch against your iPhone and Régleur listens to it tick: rate in seconds a day, beat error, beat rate and an amplitude estimate, explained in plain English. Then it keeps your collection and its service history in one calm place.

  • PlatformiPhone and iPad
  • PriceFree, optional Pro subscription

partsbin

Printable parts from parameters

Beta

Design a 3D-printable part by typing its dimensions, watch the solid update as you go, then export it. Real B-rep geometry, so the STEP file you get is a solid model you can keep editing in FreeCAD or Fusion, not a bag of triangles.

  • PlatformBrowser, nothing to install
  • PriceFree
  • ExportsSTEP · SCAD · STL · 3MF
  • Your dataStays in your browser
