The studio

Different problems, same standards

These tools have nothing to do with each other. One watches an API bill, the other cuts printable geometry. What they share is that we use them ourselves, daily, in the work and the hobbies they came out of. Each one exists because nothing else did the job the way we wanted it done, and there will be more for the same reason.

It runs where you are

Your work stays on your machine or in your browser. There is no server holding your spend history or your designs, because there is no server.

No account, no funnel

Nothing to sign up for and nothing to cancel. Analytics are off until you say otherwise, and saying no means we never contact them at all.

You keep the output

A CSV, a STEP file, a number you can check against your invoice. Everything these tools produce is yours in a format that outlives them.

Honest about the limits

Estimates are labelled as estimates, gaps are written down, and where a tool is not good enough yet it says so. Read the accessibility notes for the version of that we can't hide from.
