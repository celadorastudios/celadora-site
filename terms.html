The numbers are estimates, not your bill

Claude-o-Meter tells you what your Claude Code usage probably cost. It is not a billing system, and its numbers are not authoritative.

It works by reading the usage logs Claude Code writes on your own machine and multiplying them by a pricing table stored on your machine. That table can fall out of date the moment a provider changes prices; your logs may be incomplete, rotated or missing; and your actual invoice depends on discounts, credits, taxes, minimums and billing rules the app cannot see. The only authoritative record of what you owe is the invoice from your provider: Anthropic, AWS, or whoever bills you.

The same applies to everything the app infers. Its insight cards, model recommendations, savings estimates and month-end projections are heuristics computed from local logs. They are suggestions, not advice, and not predictions. Statements about the relative cost, speed or quality of any model are general observations, not guarantees about your workload. Nothing the app says is financial, tax, accounting, procurement or professional advice, and you must not rely on it as such.

Do not use Claude-o-Meter as the sole basis for a spending decision, a budget commitment, a client bill or a chargeback. Check your provider's own billing dashboard before anything financial depends on the number.
