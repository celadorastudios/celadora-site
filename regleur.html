A Celadora Studios app for iPhone and iPad

Listen to your watch. Know how it runs.

Régleur turns your iPhone or iPad into a timegrapher. Rest a mechanical watch against it and see the rate in seconds a day, the beat error, the beat rate and an amplitude estimate, explained in plain English. Then keep your collection and its service history in one calm place.

App Store link coming soon See how it works

In testing

Régleur is in testing now and coming to the App Store. It is free to download, with an optional Pro subscription. A phone microphone is not a bench instrument: rate and beat error are usually reliable, and amplitude is an estimate. See the Terms of use.

What it does

01

Measure

Rate in seconds a day, beat error, beat rate and an amplitude estimate. Early readings appear within seconds, shown with a "give or take" that narrows as Régleur listens, plus a plain-English summary of how the watch is running.

02

Collection

Keep every watch with photos, movement details and its measurement history. Optional background cleanup for watch photos runs entirely on your device.

03

Care

Record service dates and intervals and follow trends over time, so you know when a watch needs attention. Reminders are notifications scheduled by your own device.

Your watch, your data

No account, no ads and no tracking. Régleur analyzes the sound of your watch live on your device and never records or uploads audio. Your collection stays on your device, and we run no server that receives it. Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple, so we never see your payment details. If that ever changes, the privacy policy changes first.
