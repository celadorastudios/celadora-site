A Celadora Studios app for iPhone and iPad

Know your movement.

Régleur listens to your mechanical watch through the iPhone microphone and shows how it is running: rate in seconds a day, beat error, beat rate and an amplitude estimate, explained in plain English. Hold the bottom of your phone close to the watch and a first reading appears in seconds. Then keep every watch, its readings and its service dates in one calm collection.

App Store link coming soon See how it works

In testing

Régleur is in testing now and coming to the App Store. It is free to download, with an optional Pro subscription. A phone microphone is not a bench instrument: rate and beat error are usually reliable, and amplitude is an estimate. See the Terms of use.

What it does

01

Measure

Rate, beat error, beat rate and an amplitude estimate, measured live. Early readings appear within seconds, with a "give or take" that narrows as Régleur listens, and a plain-English summary of what the numbers mean. It works on the wrist, in your hand or on a table, and a Demo Watch lets you try everything without a watch nearby.

02

Keep your collection

Save each watch with its brand, model, reference, calibre, beat rate and tolerance. Keep readings, notes, service dates and documents together, with one clear status for each watch and how it has been running over time. Photo cleanup cuts out the watch, straightens and frames it, and places it on a backdrop that suits its colors, all on your device.

03

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Audio is analyzed on your iPhone and never saved or uploaded. Your collection stays on your device, and your display settings can follow you through your own iCloud.

Your watch, your data

No account, no ads and no tracking. Régleur analyzes the sound of your watch live on your device and never records or uploads audio. Your collection stays on your device, and we run no server that receives it. Display settings can sync through your own iCloud if you leave that on. Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple, so we never see your payment details. If that ever changes, the privacy policy changes first.
